About TAMESHE Global Nigeria

A trusted name in Kano's textile trade — built on quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction.

Our Story

From a single stall to a trusted textile destination

TAMESHE Global Nigeria began with a simple goal: bring dependable, high-quality fabric to the families, tailors and traders of Kano State. Over the years, our commitment to quality, trust, integrity and customer satisfaction has grown our showroom into a full-scale retail and wholesale operation, stocking everything from everyday cotton to bridal lace.

Today, we serve individuals, fashion designers, boutique owners and corporate clients across Northern Nigeria and beyond, backed by an organized warehouse and a team that treats every customer like family.

Our Mission

To provide premium, affordable textiles and reliable service to every customer — from a single yard to bulk wholesale orders.

Our Vision

To be Northern Nigeria's most trusted textile retailer, known for quality, variety and exceptional customer care.

Our Core Values

Quality, Trust, Integrity and Customer Satisfaction guide every decision we make, from sourcing to sale.
