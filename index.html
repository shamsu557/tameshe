Our Story

Rooted in Kano, trusted across Nigeria

TAMESHE Global Nigeria is a textile retail business based in Farawa Ibrahim Kunya, Kano State, dedicated to bringing premium fabrics to families, tailors, designers and traders. Our showroom and warehouse carry curated Ankara, Lace, Brocade, Silk and Cotton collections, sourced and inspected for lasting quality.
